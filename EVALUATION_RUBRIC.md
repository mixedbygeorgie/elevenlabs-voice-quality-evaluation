# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Add short timestamped notes; the number alone is not enough.

| Dimension | 1 — Unusable | 3 — Usable with work | 5 — Production-ready |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity / timbre | Voice identity changes or sounds synthetic | Recognizable but inconsistent | Convincing, stable identity |
| Intelligibility | Words are difficult to understand | Mostly clear with isolated issues | Clear throughout |
| Pronunciation | Frequent or distracting errors | Some correctable issues | Natural and contextually correct |
| Prosody / phrasing | Flat, erratic or inappropriate | Generally plausible | Intentional, natural and engaging |
| Emotion / performance | Emotion is absent or mismatched | Partly preserves intent | Preserves or supports intended performance |
| Artifact level | Repeated digital, breath or transition artifacts | Several repairable artifacts | No distracting artifacts |
| Stability | Character shifts within or between takes | Minor variation | Consistent within and across takes |
| Editability | Difficult to cut, process or match | Usable after moderate cleanup | Easy to edit and integrate |
| Source sensitivity | Fails under small input changes | Noticeable but manageable impact | Robust under tested conditions |
| Overall production usability | Cannot be delivered | Viable with engineering | Ready or near-ready for delivery |

## Review questions

- What would a customer notice first?
- What could be fixed in editing, and what requires regeneration?
- Did the model preserve the performance or only the words?
- Which issue is systematic, and which is take-specific?
- Would the result survive music, sound design or localization context?

## Scoring note

Scores are subjective expert judgments from a small controlled evaluation. Report them as observations, not universal product claims.
