# Application Answer

I built a small multilingual listening evaluation of ElevenLabs Instant Voice Cloning using Eleven Flash v2.5. I compared two practical source-capture profiles across English, Norwegian and Spanish while keeping the model and voice settings constant within each language pair.

The challenged condition reduced the composite score from 3.71 to 3.33, primarily because the mean artifact score fell from 4.00 to 2.67. More importantly, all six outputs scored 1/5 for identity. One Spanish output was technically deliverable as a generic synthetic voice, but neither clone was acceptable as a faithful reproduction of my voice.

The exercise reinforced a production principle I use in professional audio work: technical cleanliness and identity fidelity must be evaluated separately, with identity treated as a hard gate in voice-cloning QA. I documented the method, rubric, scores, timestamped findings, production implications and limitations in the repository below.
